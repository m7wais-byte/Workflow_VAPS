## Backup and restore drill
- **Backup:** taken with the app stopped (`scripts/backup/backup.mjs --local`): 17 tables, 183 rows and 2 files, with a manifest of row counts and SHA-256 checksums.
- **Restore:** into a fresh, separate Docker volume (`scripts/backup/restore.mjs --local`). Every table's row count matched, and both files read back from storage matched byte for byte.
- **Refusal check:** a second restore into the now non-empty database was refused.
- **Restored copy:** started on port 3001 with "No migrations to apply". Compared with the original through the API, it matched on:
  - sign-in for 5 users;
  - job revision 44 and its 29 history entries;
  - file downloads with identical checksums;
  - the same permission refusals.
- **Not tested:** the same scripts with `--remote` against Cloudflare (there is no account yet).
